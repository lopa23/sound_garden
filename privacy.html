Sound Garden – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Sound Garden is a phonics practice app for children. We built it to collect nothing.

  • No personal information is collected. There are no accounts, sign-ins, names, emails or contact details.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app has no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics.
  • No microphone or camera. The app does not record audio or video.
  • Progress stays on the device. Star counts and practice progress are saved only in the app's local storage on your device and are never sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data removes them.
  • Support link. The optional “Support us” button sits behind a grown-up check and opens PayPal, an outside website with its own privacy policy. The app shares no information with it.
  • Speech. Words are spoken using your device's built-in text-to-speech voice.

Because no data is collected, none is shared or sold. The app is designed to comply with children's privacy laws such as COPPA.

Questions: please open an issue at github.com/lopa23/sound_garden.
